Dig, build and explore an endless pixel world.

PokMiner is a free 2D mining and crafting game for Windows. It's in alpha, so new updates land often (and so do the bugs made by Cacto).

What's new

What you can do

Mine deep

Dig through stone, deepslate and Goredstone layers, from coal and iron down to diamonds and rarer ores.

Craft and smelt

Discover new recipes, build a crafting table, smelt ores in a furnace and forge gear at the anvil.

Endless world

9 biomes, from birch forests to red deserts and snowy spruce forests. The world keeps generating as you explore.

Skill tree

Spend XP on Mining and Fighting skills. Every branch makes you choose a path for good.

Play with friends

Host a world on your local network or join a friend by address. Chat, explore together or fight it out.

Missions

Cave Survival, Parkour, Dungeon Exploration and Escape Room missions earn you coins for the shop.

Rings and pendants

Equip accessories that boost your health, mining speed, regeneration or jumps.

Music discs

Collect music discs and play them in a jukebox, even with friends in multiplayer.

How to install

  1. Download

    Click Download. You get a single .exe file, no installer needed.

  2. Run it

    Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game isn't signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Play

    Keep the file somewhere handy, like your Desktop. The main menu tells you when a new version is out.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • Keyboard and mouse
  • Languages: English, Polski

Some antivirus programs wrongly flag single-file games like this one. Only download PokMiner from this page or the official GitHub releases.
