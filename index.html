What's new

What you can do

Mine deep

Dig through stone, deepslate and Goredstone layers, from coal and iron down to diamonds and rarer ores.

Craft and smelt

Discover new recipes, build a crafting table, smelt ores in a furnace and forge gear at the anvil.

Endless world

9 biomes, from birch forests to red deserts and snowy spruce forests. The world keeps generating as you explore.

Skill tree

Spend XP on Mining and Fighting skills. Every branch makes you choose a path for good.

Play with friends

Host a world on your local network or join a friend by address. Chat, explore together or fight it out.

Missions

Cave Survival, Parkour, Dungeon Exploration and Escape Room missions earn you coins for the shop.

Rings and pendants

Equip accessories that boost your health, mining speed, regeneration or jumps.

Music discs

Collect music discs and play them in a jukebox, even with friends in multiplayer.

How to install

  1. Download

    Choose your system above and download either the game or the launcher.

  2. Extract and run

    Extract the downloaded archive, then open the game or launcher inside. On Linux, you may need to run chmod +x PokMiner for the game.

  3. Play

    The launcher can install and update the game. If you run the game directly, its main menu tells you when a new version is out.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 (32- or 64-bit), Windows 11 (64-bit), macOS (Intel or Apple Silicon), or Linux (64-bit x86)
  • Keyboard and mouse
  • Languages: English, Polski

Windows or macOS may warn that the app is unsigned; only open it if you trust the source. Only download PokMiner from this page or the official GitHub releases.
